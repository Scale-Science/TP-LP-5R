The saliva gap

You're Not Getting Cavities Because You Brush Badly. You're Getting Them Because Nothing Is Working In Between.

Brushing takes four minutes a day. Your mouth runs for the other sixteen.

One thing defends your teeth in those sixteen hours: saliva. It neutralizes acid after you eat. It washes bacteria off enamel. It carries minerals back to surfaces acid just stripped. When saliva is flowing, your mouth cleans itself between meals. That is the system working.

The problem is that the system keeps getting shut off.

Coffee is acidic, and caffeine cuts saliva production, so the acid sits on your teeth instead of getting rinsed away. Snacking every couple of hours restarts the acid cycle before your mouth has recovered from the last one. Stress, medication, and mouth breathing suppress saliva directly. And for a lot of women in their late thirties and forties, hormonal shifts reduce it further, which is why dry mouth and sensitivity seem to show up together around the same time.

When saliva drops, it is not one problem. Bacteria multiply. Acid lingers. Enamel softens. Gums get irritated. Four things going wrong at once.

Questions

Before You Order

Is Toothpod safe for kids?
Toothpod is formulated for ages 12 and up. It is sugar-free, aspartame-free, vegan, non-GMO and third-party tested every batch, and it works with braces and aligners. Like any chewing gum, it is not suitable for young children because of choking risk. Our advisory team includes a pediatric dentist.
Is xylitol safe? I've heard it's dangerous for dogs.
Xylitol is genuinely toxic to dogs, and that is worth taking seriously. Keep Toothpod out of reach of pets exactly as you would keep chocolate, grapes or gum of any kind. For humans, xylitol is a naturally derived sweetener used in oral care for decades and recommended by dentists specifically because decay bacteria cannot metabolize it. The gut complaints you may have read about come from very large daily quantities, far beyond what is in a piece of gum.
How many should I chew a day?
Two minimum. Protection is timed to the window, not accumulated across the day, so one piece covers one window. Two covers your two biggest: after morning coffee and after lunch. Chew a third after dinner or any snack if you want fuller coverage. A 3-pack is 60 pieces, which is a full month at two a day.
Can this replace brushing or flossing?
No, and we would not want it to. Toothpod covers the sixteen hours brushing was never designed to reach. Brush morning and night, and use Toothpod in between.
Will it help with sensitivity?
Sensitivity is usually an enamel problem, not a nerve problem. Sensitivity toothpaste works on the nerve signal. Toothpod works on the enamel, using hydroxyapatite, the same mineral enamel is made of. Most people notice a difference in the three to five month range with consistent use.
Will it whiten my teeth?
Toothpod is not a whitening product and contains no peroxide. What it does is help smooth the enamel surface, and a smoother surface gives coffee, tea and wine less to grip. Several customers report their teeth looking brighter over time. That is a downstream effect of enamel health, not bleaching, so set your expectations accordingly.
What does it feel like to chew?
Firmer than a stick of gum. It is a tablet, not candy, and it softens as you chew. Flavour holds through the full 10 to 15 minutes, which is the point, because that is how long the actives take to release.
